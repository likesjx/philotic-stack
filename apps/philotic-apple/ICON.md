## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iPhone app icon for Philotic, a personal connected-agent app with life context, location and health. Create one finished square icon artwork, not a presentation or device mockup. An original, beautifully restrained emblem of connected intelligence: three broad softly luminous filaments interweave into a compact sculptural knot, with a small warm light at their shared center. Strong simple iconic silhouette, generous negative space, clearly legible at 48 pixels. Elegant tactile depth, satin ceramic or softly frosted glass rather than shiny chrome; precise smooth edges, controlled soft highlights, no excessive bloom. Calm deep ink-blue full-bleed background, the mark in pale luminous ice-blue with a subtle warm ivory center. Centered emblem occupies about 65 percent of square width. Premium understated native Apple app aesthetic. No lettering, no words, no brain, no robot, no medical cross, no map pin, no atom or orbital paths, no starfield, no extra ornaments. Fully opaque, edge-to-edge square image at 1024 by 1024 pixels. Do not bake in rounded corners or draw an outer tile border; iOS applies its own mask. Output only the single icon image.
