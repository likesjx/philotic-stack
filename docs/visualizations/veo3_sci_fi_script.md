# Philotic Engine: The 3-Shot Sci-Fi Video Script

Since you are capped at exactly 3 generations per day with Veo 3.1, this script compresses the core Philotic metaphors—**The Canonical Hotel, The Router Boundary, and Guest Materialization**—into three densely packed, dynamic 8-second scenes. 

Google Veo responds best to extreme specificity in **subject motion, materials, lighting, and camera behavior**. These prompts are engineered to perfectly leverage its strengths to produce a continuous 24-second narrative.

---

### Scene 1: The Boot & The Mesh (0:00 - 0:08)
**Narrative Goal:** Establish the canonical Hotel daemon materializing and instantly connecting to the distributed Ansible mesh.

**Veo 3.1 Prompt:**
> Cinematic 8k, vibrant cyberpunk caricature style. A massive, slightly exaggerated monolithic building labeled "HOTEL ANSIBLE" in glowing magenta neon rapidly constructs itself out of a dark, grid-lined digital void. As soon as the building takes shape, the camera rapidly orbits it as it physically shoots out thick, pulsing, cyan fiber-optic cables across the void. The camera follows one cable instantly to reveal it plugging into a distant, identical building glowing green. The background fills with a sprawling, glowing spider-web network of distinct, vibrant hotel buildings. Fluid continuous motion, dramatic volumetric lighting, highly stylized high-scale science fiction.

---

### Scene 2: The Cross-Section & The Context Fetch (0:08 - 0:16)
**Narrative Goal:** Push through the boundary to reveal the Router and Guest Membranes, and show an isolated Agent waking up to request its state.

**Veo 3.1 Prompt:**
> Cinematic 8k, vibrant cyberpunk caricature style. The camera pushes seamlessly through the solid wall of the "HOTEL ANSIBLE" building, revealing a cavernous interior cross-section. Floating in the space are thick glass containment rooms ("GUEST MEMBRANES") physically cabled to a massive, central spinning mechanical sphere (the "ROUTER"). Inside one of the glass rooms, a quirky, stylized robot slouched in a chair suddenly wakes up, its bright cyan mechanical eyes snapping open. The robot slams its hand on the glass wall, causing a thick, blindingly bright beam of golden data to shoot out from the room along the cable directly into the central swirling Router. Tight camera framing, intense high-contrast neon lighting, smooth focus pull.

---

### Scene 3: The Handoff & Execution (0:16 - 0:24)
**Narrative Goal:** The Router feeds context back, the Agent accepts its Role, and physically transfers into execution space.

**Veo 3.1 Prompt:**
> Cinematic 8k, vibrant cyberpunk caricature style. Inside the glass containment room, the quirky stylized robot is struck by a blinding beam of golden data returning from the Router, illuminating it with immense power. The robot grinningly slaps a glowing holographic "ROLE" badge onto its metallic chest. Without hesitation, the glowing robot leaps forward into a massive, swirling wormhole-like portal at the back of its containment room, leaving behind a trail of sparking physical code blocks and binary digits that dissipate in the air. The camera tilts up as the portal bursts with intense magenta energy. Triumphant, highly kinetic camera movement, bright over-the-top sci-fi action, sharp detail.
